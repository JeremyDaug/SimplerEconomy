# Current tree

Working note for the fresh start. Read this before editing the library.

`Design_V3.md` is the design source of truth. The user owns it. Do not edit it. It is an intent document, not a code spec, and it is still being written. Where it is silent, leave the gap. `docs/Overview.md` and `TODO.md` describe the previous tree.

## What this crate is

Rust 2024 library, crate `simpler_economy`. No library binary. No Bevy in simulation code. `std` collections. `hexx` for the hex map.

World data is `data/world/goods.toml`, `data/world/processes.toml`, and `data/world/config.toml`. The load path is [loaders.md](loaders.md).

`cargo test --lib` is the check.

`examples/pop_tester` runs the pops in `data/pop_tester` through `PlayState::advance_turn`. It loads `data/world`, then `desires.toml`, `species.toml`, `cultures.toml`, `pops.toml`, and `market.toml` from that folder. Good and process names resolve against the world. A species or culture desire is derived from a platonic desire. The first argument is the day count, default 1. `cargo run --example pop_tester`.

## Live

Goods, processes, and households are the live systems.

`Good::load_from_toml` and `Process::load_from_toml` load world data. `TIME` is good id `0`. `Process::do_process` and `do_process_leg` stay, including recipe bonuses and the side-effect arms (development, unity, faith, authority, legitimacy, birth, mortality). Those arms are Unity and Development. Do not start a strip of `process.rs` unless asked. Subsistence in code is still `complexity < 1`. The pin's subsistence process is the dynamic special process, and pops do not use complexity. Desire `decay` uses the good's scale. 0 keeps satisfaction and 1 clears it. Kept satisfaction is multiplied by `1.0 - decay`. `Pop::decay_desires` applies that.

`Household`, `BirthSentiment`, `DemographicRates`, and `HouseholdTarget` stay whole, including their methods and tests. `Household::update` reads `PopConfig` for childhood years, adulthood years, the birth sex ratio, and the partnership step. `HouseholdTarget` is also what processes store. `Household` calls `lerp` in `src/game/util.rs`.

`Factuals` loads a world folder from goods, then processes, then config. It keeps maps for species and cultures. A world folder does not load those maps.

`GameConfig` sections are `pop`, `player_resources`, `market`, `deal`, `market_priority`, `labor`, and `firm`. `pop`, `market`, and `labor` hold the tunables in [loaders.md](loaders.md). `labor.time_per_labor` defaults to 16. `pop.turn_length` defaults to 1 and must be at least 1. `pop.subsistence_anchor` defaults to 0.6, `pop.subsistence_common_share` defaults to 0.5, and `pop.desire_share_minimum_divisor` defaults to 2. It is 0 or at least 1. Consumption reads it from config. The other sections are empty. Missing keys keep `Default`.

`Map` stores the hex grid, wrapping, regions, and city hexes. `add_city` and `add_to_region` are in. The map tests cover that.

## Present, not a day loop

These types are in the tree. Keep the fields. Add a method only when asked.

`PlayState` holds factuals, map data, actors, players, the turn, and market lookups. `advance_turn` increments the turn and calls one method per step in the design turn order. Start of day grants morning time. Apply player demographic changes then matches each existing pop desire to its demographic desire. A changed amount is written as that amount times the pop's current scale. A bucket whose goods differ is replaced. Satisfaction stays. Bonuses in that step are not written. Intra-market day runs each market on its own thread. Inside a market, pops run subsistence, post sell offers, then early player, institutions, firms, pops, and late player trade, then the market records the day's trades. Subsistence spends `subsistence_rate` of the pop's time and adds non-industrial goods. Basic cover is the rate divided by `pop.subsistence_anchor`, and common cover is that times `pop.subsistence_common_share`, with equal satisfaction from each good in a desire. The offer split reads tender and exchange rates from the market history, then reserves job inputs up to the plan, then basic and common desires. Savings after a finished tier is a total measured in days of basic desires: 1 after basic, 3 after common, and 5 after the first luxury level, plus 2 days for each luxury level after that. Currency already saved counts. Luxury and that savings step alternate until the tier cannot finish or the savings total cannot be reached. Unreserved property is the sell offer. Pop buys walk desires in tier order, wealthiest household first. A desire takes the offered good with the lowest cost per satisfaction in the market's first currency, in whole units, paid from loose tender and then savings. A pop does not buy its own offer, the tender, or time. Trade records are not written. Production calls each pop, firm, and institution on its own thread. A pop runs its job lines and does not run subsistence. Effects on the process result are stored on the pop. Firm and institution production are not written. Consumption runs each pop on its own thread. A pop satisfies basic, then common, then luxury, and does not start a tier until covered satisfaction reaches the tier before it. Each bucket good is brought toward `Desire::minimum`, then efficiency fills the rest up to `Desire::cap`. Those read `pop.desire_share_minimum_divisor` from config. The implicit minimum is `1 / (divisor * goods)` and the implicit cap is `1 / divisor` plus that share. The stored minimum can only raise the share, and the stored cap can only lower the cap. A divisor of 0 uses the stored values alone. Luxury repeats until a level cannot be finished. Growth, bonus-good, and player-resource effects are then stored on the pop. Satisfaction and sentiment stay on the desire. A bonus scales with levels met. A malus scales with the unmet share of one level and stops once that level is met. Luxury player resources keep the extra levels. Other arms stop at one level. Growth runs births and deaths on each pop's thread. `Pop::growth_phase` stacks demographic rates, dispenses stored birth and mortality effects, scales births by sentiment, and calls `Household::update` with `pop.turn_length`. A household count under 1 skips that tick. A partnership of 0 on a rate bundle is not a rate. The defined side is kept, and the default is 2.5 when neither side names one. Cap rebalance multiplies the household counts of capped pops in a market by one factor when their people exceed the sum of their caps. Prune unlists a pop under one household and records `PopExit::UnderOneHousehold`, then drops the actor. Local migration clears `Pop::migration` and reads the market's region hexes. The same-hex offer is not written. Regional migration runs after local, and inter-market migration runs after that. Those two passes are not written. Decay calls `Actors::decay`, which runs each pop and each firm on its own thread. A pop returns `used` to `quantity`, then decays aging stock by the good's rate. Fresh units stay. An owned exposure good stays whole. Byproducts are credited, and `lost` records the rot. A stored bonus good is added and removed. Each desire keeps satisfaction times `1.0 - decay`. `MapData::decay_goods` is still unwritten. Rescale runs each pop on its own thread. Growth records the pre-growth population, and rescale multiplies each set plan target and each desire amount by the current population over that record. An open plan stays open. Satisfaction stays. Record keeping runs each market on its own thread, then `Actors::record_keeping` runs each pop and then each firm, each on its own thread. A market replaces production, consumption, and decay from the pops and firms it counts. A member listed on several markets is counted by the lowest market id. Prices then move one good at a time. The stored rate is goods per currency unit, and the step prices the good in that currency. `sought` against `offered` is the one rise or fall. `market.price_gain` defaults to 0.10 and `market.price_cap` to 0.20. Each currency keeps a count, and its weight is that count times the morning rate. The move is split by weight. A sole trader absorbs 95% and a currency that did not trade absorbs the other 5%. Equal weights stay equal. A good with volume 0 takes the dearest method's input cost plus 10%. A pop stores the sum of desire satisfaction, or infinity when it has no desires. It copies the time row's consumed for planning, then clears the day's flow counters. The firm record keeper is not written. Planning calls `Actors::planning`, which runs each pop, firm, and institution on its own thread. A pop keeps yesterday's used time plus 5% of its morning grant, once for desires and once for haul. Desire time is the copied consumption of time. Haul time is `transport_spent` minus `transport_purchased`, and not below 0. A hold below that goal rises to it. A hold above it steps down by 5% of the grant. The two holds shrink when they ask for more than the grant. Craft and labor are value per unit of time. A craft's value is its non-time output value minus its required non-time input value, divided by the time one run requires. Optional, factor, and capital inputs are left out of that cost. A process tagged Labor uses its non-time output value divided by that time. A line with no required time has value 0. The line that leads and beats the anchor subsistence bundle steps up by 5% of the grant. A tie at that lead also steps up. Every other line steps down by one step, and not straight to 0. Downs run first. Subsistence takes the time those lines leave, and not more than `subsistence_anchor` of the morning grant. Sentiments runs each pop on its own thread against the lowest market that lists it. Desire mood arms and stored mood arms apply together with the tier flats, wealth per household in the market's first currency, the change in living over the last five readings, and a pull of each other emotion toward contentment. Today's living is recorded after that, and stored satisfaction is then dropped. Firm and institution planning are not written. The other step methods are empty.

`Pop` is one household block, a `Job`, property rows, savings, desires, sentiment, and a demographic row of species and culture. `transport_spent` and `transport_purchased` are yesterday's haul. `time_consumed` is the time row's consumption copied at record keeping. `Job` is the cottage lines: a process, a target, and the optional inputs that line may draw. `Pop::produce` runs those lines. `Pop::consume` spends goods on hand toward the pop's desires.

`Desire` has platonic, demographic, and pop desires, with constructors, `set_amount`, `create_desire`, and target ordering.

`Market` holds members, goods, and price history. `MarketHistory::from_config` reads `salability_default`.

`Firm` is one workshop: owners, workforce, property rows, and production lines. `Firm::decay_goods` rots aging stock and the decay step calls it. `MapData` stores the map and markets. `market` and `market_mut` are the getters. `MapData::decay_goods` panics. Growth calls `process_inter_market_migration` and then `process_organized_migration`. Their bodies are empty.

`Species` and `Culture` keep their fields and desire lists. `Sentiment` is the five-way partition, with its tests. `Institution`, `Workforce`, `PlayerResources`, `Players`, `Nation`, effects, `ScalingFactor`, `Tile`, and `Region` keep their fields. `Plot` is the terrain enum. `Technology` is a node with id, cost, parents, and unlock lists. `TechTree` is empty. `init` is the module comment for scenario kickoff.

`Unit` and `FirmOrganization` are empty shells.

`src/game/util.rs` has `random_index`, `lerp`, and `whole_units_up`. `lerp` is used by `Household`. The other two have no caller.

`#![allow(dead_code)]` is on `desire`, `job`, `market`, `pop`, and `species`.

`DemographicSource` is species, culture, or religion. `Factuals` has no religion map. `source_demo_desire` returns `None` for a religion source.
