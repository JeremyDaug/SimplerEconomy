# Factuals

## Goods

Goods are those things which are traded. They are produced, used, consumed, bought, sold, and destroyed. They are fixed at game start, being considered open to modding.

Goods include a unique ID and name, a class which defines groupings of highly similar goods (currently not used), a decay rate (In * Rate => Out) and decay result (what it becomes upon decaying), a mass and volume (used for Bulk calculations), a list of tags for more complex behavior and rules, and categories, used for searching and organization.

### Good Types

- Raw Resources, simple, often extracted or refinedfdrom the environment, little to no decay.
- Crafts, common crafts, things anyone could do with a few tools at most.
- Industrial Goods, highly advanced, capital intensive goods that really cannot be easily produced in small businesses.
- Skills, specific skills used to improve the work done in specific jobs.
- Labors, specific kinds of work, used to interface time and skills to more advanced/specialized work and is what is typically traded from pops to more advanced firms.
- Transportation, can be freely and instantly converted into some amount of Transport at any time (should only do as needed).
- Currency, goods denoted as currency are allowed to take on currency status. They can be traded in smaller units (1/1000th of a full unit). Only really ment for goods that are inherently highly fungible (raw resources, specifically designed goods, etc).
- Maintenance, a class of goods, similar to labor, but is used to reduce or remove decay from goods like factories.

## Special Goods

- Time, created each morning, lost each night, and spent between the two. Generated by pops mostly. It is one of the most foundational goods and goes into almost everything.
- Transport, the unit of moving stuff around. 1 Transport moves 1 person 1 Hex along the map assuming no terrain slows things down. It also moves (TBR) 1 Bulk of goods
- Plots, Tied to the map. Plots are the smallest unit of land abstract land. They are partnered with secondary features which can be traded away to some extent.

## Processes

Processes are actions that transform goods into other things.

### Process Types

- Internal, special processes available to anyone/everyone and used to do trivial transformations that have complex inputs or some greater cost. IE, Transportation.
- Agricultural, typically include a plot of land, a starter input (seed grain) or existing capital (orchards), and the right climate.
- Extraction, typically some form of resource extraction from the environment that's not inherently sustainable (lumber, oil extraction, mining, etc) and requires special plots of land to work with.
- Craft, A simple production that is doable as a cottage industry, even if it requires some additional tools or space.
- Industrial, a complex process that cannot be done by hand and typically requires heavy capital to work.
- Maintenance, a process which is allowed to consume used capital and refresh it, dodging it's decay.

# Actors

An Actor is any 'animate' thing that can operate in the game. This is mostly directed to Pops, Firms, Institutions, and Nation. Actors are any entity in the system which can own property and interact with the market.

## Pops

Pops are the lowest kind of actor, representing people themselves. They are the primary source of the ultimate resource, time, and are the end consumer. They are also the laborer and owners.

Pops are divided by location, demographics, craft, and labor. They can be subdivided further to represent specific pops (owners of private firms, political actors, etc).

### Pop Household

A pop's smallest workable unit is the Household, which is divided by age (Child, Adult, Elder) and sex (Male, Female). They are grouped together to form a 'household'. Each category can have a different aspects and be used for different scaling if we so choose. For now though, they are mostly for demographic purposes.

### Pop Property

Pops have 2 kinds of property, goods and shares. 

Goods are the goods from above and represent what they own in that sense. Typically, they should only own those goods they need to satisfy their desires, goods for their jobs (craft inputs, skills, time), and currencies. 

Shares are shares of ownership in other things. At game start, that's just land, but later on it includes citizenship, and ownership of firms. Ownership can be bought and sold to varying degrees based on what is owned and by what mechanism it is being owned.

### Pop Work

Pops have 3 primary factors to them: Subsistence Factor, Craft, and Labor.

Subsistence is a value from 0 to 1 which defines how much of their daily time they put towards the Subsistence Process. Subsistence output is guaranteed to the pop's personal use and cannot be traded under normal circumstances.

Craft are a singular 'simple' process that a pop can do as an alternative income source. These produce goods that are offered for sale so a pop can purchase resources from the market.

Labor is an job they can take which allows them to convert time and skill into a specific labor that they can offer on the market like craft. It's treated separately from Craft as it distinguishes from a cottage style worker and a member of the labor force.

A pop can have one of each, with it's time divided between them in simple allocation mechanisms.

### Demographics

- Species, the biological baseline of the pop. Generally highly fixed, but can be modified in small ways. Assimilation can only occur on this metric by special means.
  - Ethnicity, the biological variance from the baseline of the pop. Assimilation can occur, but does so in line with birthrates. Slow and very difficult to change, most changes are random.
- Culture, the cultural identity of the demographic. Starts as 'everything above biology', but can diverge later with development and institutions.
  - Subculture, what it says on the tin. It's a modification of the original culture, creating a new subculture. These cover everything from Class, to Caste.
- Religion, the spiritual identity of the demographic. Tied to a faith and easier to transmit or convert than culture.
- Ideology, the 'highest' level of identity. Tied in heavily with mass politics.

Only Species and Culture will be worked on at the moment. All others can come later as they should be mostly based on those.

### Desires

Pops needs are called desires and come in a 3 tier set. Basic, Common, and Luxury. Basic desires are those needed to keep the pop alive. If they have none of them, they are staving and will begin dying shortly thereafter. Common desires are those things that are considerde 'necissary' for a respectable life for that pop. Luxury needs are those which a pop wants and can always have more of.

Desires have a bucket of goods they seek out to satisfy them and include an efficiency factor for it. The exact amount of satisfaciton needed can scale with any part or division of the household.

Desires have a list of goods in them called a bucket which attaches an efficiency rating to each and defines how units convert into satisfaction on a per-unit basis. 

What goods are allowed in this bucket are defined by the Platonic Desire, which defines how other desires in a game are allowed to look. They are converted into Demographic desires which are a attached to demographics and define what the that demographic is working with specifically. That is then converted down into a Desire, which is attached to a pop and is scaled correctly to match that pop.

By default, every good in a bucket has a minimum requirement of `1 / 2 * Goods in the bucket`. A pop is not required to hit that minimum, but it must try to satsify that to the best of their ability. Desires may have goods added or removed by the player, but a desire must always have at least 1 good. If a player wants to swap out the last good of a desire, there will be an intermediary period where both goods are allowed at the same time as the pop moves over. That transition is allowed to take (TBR) 10 turns.

## Firms

A firm is a special entity which acts as a focal point of production distinct from a pop. They are almost purely economic actors and are mostly independent from the players in terms of their logic and actions.

### Firm Types

- Producers, focus on a set of processes, typically with an emphasis on a small set of goods they wish to make, and focus on making that good to the exclusion of everything else.
- Merchants, focus on storage, maintenance, and arbitrage of common trade goods, especially arbitrage between currencies in the local market. 
- Traders, focus on transporting goods between markets, gaining arbitrage in that fashion.

## Institutions

Institutions are organizations that operate semi-independently from the players. They have a structure that paralells the Firm, and operate for their own goals and reasons, but if kept happy, will make you stronger and give bonuses.

## Nation

Nations are the Players of the game (both human and Computer) and represent a people, their governments, and institutions. While it will be treated as effectively Synonymous with a State or Government, it includes more abstract or immaterial things like the culture, religion, and institutions. The player is effectively the "Spirit of the Nation", having influence over the lands, peoples, institutions, and governments of these nations.

# Market and Map

## Map

The map is a hexgrid with edgewrapping, similar to Civilization 5-6 and Millenia. Each Hex is also known as a Tile, and every tile has it's own climate data and it's own populations.

## Tiles

Tiles are individual hexes on the map, in includes fixed environmental data at the start, and is used to define what is where.

## Regions and Markets

Regions and Markets are Synonymous logically, but slightly different in code representation, with Regions representing the tiles on the map, and the Market representing the people and resources collected into the region. 

Markets are effectively the smallest market unit in the game. It is where actors trade between each other, and it is the primary manager of local economies. Firms, pops, and the like have hex locations which should be contained in the market and 

Markets connect to other regions for trade. 

### How the Market works

The market includes a list of prices in the various currencies of the world. All goods get a trade rate, with currencies being allowed to divide by 1/1000 instead of 1 whole unit.

Goods on offer are offered at day start. From pops, they only offer the output of their crafts and labor. Firms are allowed to sell any of their outputs. 

When an actor goes shopping, they go with a specific good/goal in mind, defined by their needed inputs, desires, and plans. Pops buying for a desire select the good that has the lowest cost per satisfaction (unit defined by the primary (most used) currency of the market currently). For inputs to processes, they go for required goods first, then optional goods, with optional goods being excludable if including them would make the process less profitable. For traders and merchants, they look for goods that are profitable to store and maintain (maintenance good cheaper than losses of storage), and arbitrage opportunities.

The unit of account, is either the good with the highest durability, or the declared unit of account.

Sellers try to balance production, storage, decay, and selling. They want to maximize production, minimize decay, maximize selling, and keep storage balanced.

Prices move based on surplus and shortage following the formula $price_{t+1,C}=price_{t,C} \times ( 1 + k \times clamp(\frac{demand in C - supply in C}{max(demand in C, supply in C)}, c, -c))$ where $C$ is the units of currency traded, $k$ is gain/loss constant (0.05-0.15), and c is our movement caps (e.g. 20%).
